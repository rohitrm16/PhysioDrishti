# Appointment Policy — PhysioDrishti

## Booking an Appointment

Appointments can be booked through:

- **Website** — physiodrishti.vercel.app → "Book a free call" or "Book consultation"
- **WhatsApp** — message our team at the number provided on the site
- **Dashboard** — existing clinic partners can schedule sessions directly in the PhysioDrishti dashboard

A confirmation is sent on WhatsApp within 2 hours of booking. The session fee and physiotherapist details are shared in the confirmation message.

## Session Types

| Session Type | Description |
|---|---|
| **Home visit** | Physiotherapist travels to the patient's home. Available within service areas. |
| **In-person (clinic)** | Patient visits a partner clinic. Address shared on confirmation. |
| **Online video** | Video call via WhatsApp or Google Meet. Available anywhere in India. |

## Cancellation Policy

- **More than 24 hours before the session** — free cancellation or reschedule
- **Between 4 and 24 hours before** — 50% cancellation fee applies
- **Less than 4 hours before or no-show** — full session fee is charged

To cancel or reschedule, WhatsApp the team as early as possible.

## Rescheduling

A session can be rescheduled once at no charge, provided at least 12 hours' notice is given. Additional reschedules may incur a rescheduling fee.

## Late Arrival

If a patient arrives or is ready more than 15 minutes after the session start time, the session may be shortened. The full fee still applies.

## First Session

The first session typically includes:

1. A detailed assessment (history, pain mapping, functional tests)
2. A short treatment or pain relief intervention
3. A home exercise plan to start the same day

Bring any relevant reports: X-rays, MRI scans, discharge summaries from hospital.

## Follow-Up Sessions

Follow-up sessions are typically 45–60 minutes. The physiotherapist reviews progress, upgrades the exercise plan, and delivers hands-on treatment as needed.

## Payment

- Payment is due at the end of each session (or before for home visits)
- Accepted: UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm), bank transfer, cash
- Invoices are sent on WhatsApp after each session

## Privacy

Patient information is kept strictly confidential and used only to plan and deliver care. See our privacy policy on the website for full details.

## Emergency

For medical emergencies — severe fall, sudden weakness, chest pain — call 112 or go to the nearest hospital emergency room. PhysioDrishti is not an emergency service.
