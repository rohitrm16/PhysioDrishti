PhysioDrishti provides general education only and does not diagnose conditions.

Seek urgent medical care for back pain with new leg weakness, numbness in the groin or saddle area, loss of bladder or bowel control, fever, unexplained weight loss, or pain after a major fall or accident.

A physiotherapist can assess mechanical back pain and create a rehabilitation plan after a clinical consultation.
